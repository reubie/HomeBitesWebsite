Getting Started

HomeBites is an AI-powered meal planning app designed specifically for Kenyan families and households. It helps you decide what to cook each day, builds your weekly meal plan, generates grocery lists, and tracks your food budget — all guided by Mama AI, our Kenyan kitchen-savvy assistant.
Yes — HomeBites is completely free to download and use. All core features including meal planning, Mama AI, grocery lists, and budget tracking are available at no cost. We may introduce optional premium features in the future, but the core experience will always remain free.
No — you can try HomeBites in Guest Mode without signing up. Guest mode stores your data locally on your device. To sync your meal plans across devices, save your history long-term, and get a personalised experience from Mama AI, we recommend creating a free account. You can sign up with your email address or via Google/Apple sign-in.

Mama AI

Mama AI is your Kenyan cooking companion. You can ask her things like:
  • "What should I cook tonight for 4 people under KES 500?"
  • "I have ugali, sukuma wiki and tomatoes — what can I make?"
  • "Plan my meals for this week on a KES 6,000 budget."
  • "How do I make soft chapati?"
  • "Give me a healthy breakfast for my kids."
She understands Kenyan dishes, local ingredients, and budget constraints.
Mama AI requires an internet connection. If she's not responding, try these steps:
  • Check that your phone has an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
  • Close and reopen the app, then try your question again.
  • If the issue persists for more than a few minutes, our AI service may be temporarily unavailable — please try again later or email us at hello@homebites.app.
Yes. Your chat history is stored in your private, authenticated Firebase account — only you can access it. Messages are sent to our secure Cloud Function backend before reaching Google Gemini; the API key is never stored in the app itself. We do not use your chat data to train AI models. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Meal Planning

Tap the Planner tab at the bottom of the app. You'll see a 7-day calendar. Tap any meal slot (breakfast, lunch, or dinner) to add a recipe — you can search the recipe library, use a suggestion from Mama AI, or add your own. Your plan auto-generates a shopping list from the ingredients in your scheduled recipes.
Yes — use the Family Management feature in your Profile to add household members and set their dietary preferences or restrictions. Mama AI will factor these in when suggesting meals for the household.

Account & Data

Go to Profile → Edit Profile to update your name and preferences. To change your email or password, go to Profile → Settings → Account. If you signed in with Google or Apple, your email is managed by those services.
You can delete your account from Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, meal plans, grocery lists, chat history, and all associated data from our servers within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

Alternatively, email us at hello@homebites.app and we'll process the deletion manually.
