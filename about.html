Our Story

HomeBites started with a simple frustration: the exhausting daily question of "what should we cook tonight?" We built an app that answers it — intelligently, affordably, and in the context of real Kenyan households.

The problem we solve

A meal planner that actually knows Kenya

Every evening, millions of Kenyan households face the same conversation: "What are we eating?" It sounds like a small problem — but it's a daily source of stress that drains mental energy, wastes money through poor planning, and often leads to the same five dishes on repeat.

Existing meal planning apps were built for Western kitchens — filled with ingredients you can't find at the market in Nairobi, Kisumu, or Mombasa, with no awareness of Kenyan cooking traditions, budgets, or the real rhythm of how Kenyan families eat.

HomeBites fills that gap. A meal planner that knows ugali, pilau, and nyama choma as well as it knows your budget. One that thinks in Kenyan Shillings, not dollars.

Dinner should never be a source of stress. It should be something a family looks forward to.
— The HomeBites team
